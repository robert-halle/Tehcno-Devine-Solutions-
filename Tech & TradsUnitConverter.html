Privacy Policy

Tech and Trades Unit Converter
Last Updated: February 5, 2026

Your Privacy Matters: Tech and Trades Unit Converter is designed with your privacy as a top priority. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All conversions are processed locally on your device. The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect limited data as described below.

1. Information We Collect

Tech and Trades Unit Converter does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not track your browsing or usage patterns
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies

2. How the App Works

Tech and Trades Unit Converter is a fully client-side application that:

  • Runs entirely in your web browser
  • Performs all unit conversions locally on your device
  • Does not require an internet connection after initial loading
  • Does not send any data to external servers

3. Data Storage

The app may use your browser's local storage to save your preferences (such as favorite conversions or recent calculations). This data:

  • Remains stored only on your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Can be cleared at any time through your browser settings
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 AdMob Advertising

Tech and Trades Unit Converter displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. Please note:

  • Test Banners: During development and testing phases, we use AdMob test banners that do not collect real user data
  • Google AdMob: When live ads are displayed, Google AdMob may collect certain information to serve relevant advertisements. This may include:
    • Device information (device type, operating system)
    • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
    • Approximate location (based on IP address)
    • Advertising identifiers
  • Google's Privacy Policy: AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can review it at https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings or visiting Google Ads Settings

4.2 Other Third-Party Services

Aside from AdMob advertising, Tech and Trades Unit Converter does not integrate with any other third-party services, analytics platforms, or tracking networks.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

6. Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal information, there is no risk of your data being compromised. All calculations and conversions happen locally on your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

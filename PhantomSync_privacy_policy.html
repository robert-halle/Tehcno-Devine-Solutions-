Phantom Sync does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

In the event of an error or crash, the App may collect log data through third-party services. This data may include device information, operating system version, app configuration, and the time and date of the error.

Phantom Sync is not intended for children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
